[04]questions

Straight answers.

Which chains can I launch from?

Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum and BNB Chain. You pay in ETH on the first three and in BNB on BNB Chain.

Where does my coin live?

On Solana, created on pump.fun. Your deposit is carried across, the coin is created, and your first buy is made in the same launch. The tokens go to the Solana address you give.

Do I need a Solana wallet?

You need a Solana address for your tokens to land in. You don't need SOL in it.

Is fRAC1 part of pump.fun?

No. fRAC1 is independent. It launches coins on pump.fun the same way any other launch tool does.

When do launches open?

With $FRAC1. Until then this page shows live prices and the route, and takes no deposits.
